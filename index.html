The Concept

From the Shortest Route to the Most Comfortable Route.

WalkWise is a pedestrian-focused platform designed for Jeddah, that helps people explore and compare walking routes not just by distance, but by comfort. It highlights shaded paths, seating areas, nearby facilities, and accessible sidewalks, encouraging a safer, healthier, and more enjoyable walking experience in the city.
